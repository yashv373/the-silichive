the silichive

silicon + archive. a structured collection of semiconductor notes organized around the VLSI design flow. from specification to market — everything that goes into building a chip, documented in one place.

this is a living archive. entries are added and updated as i learn. not a textbook — just notes from someone trying to understand how chips are really built.
